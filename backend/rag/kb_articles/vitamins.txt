Vitamin D and Vitamin B12

Vitamin D helps the body absorb calcium and supports bone and muscle health. Low vitamin D is very common, especially with limited sun exposure, and can cause bone pain, muscle weakness, and fatigue over time. It is usually corrected with supplements and safe sun exposure, and is rarely an emergency.

Vitamin B12 is needed to make healthy red blood cells and to maintain nerve function. Low B12 can cause fatigue, weakness, numbness or tingling in the hands and feet, and memory or concentration problems. Common causes include a diet low in animal products, certain digestive conditions that reduce absorption, or some medications. Long-standing untreated deficiency can affect nerves more seriously, so it's worth addressing rather than ignoring even though it's rarely urgent.

Both deficiencies are generally straightforward to treat once identified, typically with dietary changes or supplements, and a doctor can advise on dosage and how long treatment should continue.
